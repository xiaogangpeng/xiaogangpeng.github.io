# Identity Bible — dog #1

Read off the two supplied keyframes. Every anchor below is copied into the video
prompt so the model cannot redesign the subject mid-shot.

## Species / build
- Long-haired dachshund. Seated upright, long low body, slender legs.

## Coat
- Base: rich golden-tan.
- Saddle: dark chocolate-brown over the back and across the top of the skull.
- Ears: long, heavily feathered, dark chocolate-brown, hanging below the jawline.
- Chest / throat / belly: pale cream-tan, distinctly lighter than the base.
- Tail: long and heavily feathered, dark brown fading toward a tan tip.
- Paws: light tan with individually drawn toes.

## Face
- Tan muzzle, rounded dark-brown nose.
- Thin dark mouth line; small dotted whisker marks on the muzzle.
- Amber-brown eye with a dark outline and a single small white catchlight.
- Dark-brown brow patch above the eye.

## Accessories
- **None.** No collar, tag, harness, lead or prop. Do not add any.

## Style anchors
- Cel-shaded illustration: even-weight dark brown outlines, soft airbrush
  shading inside flat colour regions, crisp edges.
- Flat backdrop, no texture, no gradient mesh, no painterly brushwork.

## Drift to reject
- Ear length or ear colour changing.
- Chest cream patch shrinking or spreading.
- Extra tail, extra paw, duplicated eye, or a second dog appearing.
- Style shifting to volumetric CGI, photoreal fur, or glossy render.
